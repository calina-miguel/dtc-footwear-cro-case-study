Test Narrative

1. Hypothesis

Traffic from comfort and arch-support ads should not land on a generic product page. It needs message-matched proof, objection handling, and a faster path to the right size/bundle.

2. Intervention

Build a Replo listicle landing page, add product comparison modules, carry UTMs into cart attributes, and test a bundle-first offer with margin guardrails.

3. Pass / Fail Rule

Ship only if revenue per visitor and contribution margin improve without checkout drop-off or paid-channel attribution loss.

Case Study Talking Points

Shopify test answer

Use this structure: traffic source -> hypothesis -> sample size -> change -> primary metric -> margin check -> decision.

High ATC, low purchase diagnosis

Likely mismatch after add-to-cart: shipping/tax shock, weak payment trust, bundle/discount conflict, size anxiety, slow checkout, or attribution leakage.

Practice most brands get wrong

They optimize conversion rate alone instead of revenue per visitor and contribution margin by channel.

Tech stack answer

Shopify, Replo, GA4, Shopify analytics, Meta Ads, UTM governance, cart attributes, heatmaps/session review, A/B testing, and COGS spreadsheets.
